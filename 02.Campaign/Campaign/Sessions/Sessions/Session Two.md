## Events

#### Waking up in the Stonehill Inn

The four adventurers awoke in their lodgings in the Stonehill Inn and arrived in the common room early in the morning. It is busy in the inn as miners and farmers break their fast on simples meals of porridge and eggs.

Sildar Hallwinter is sitting at a table in the common room and he invites the party to join him.
He explains that he is heading out of town to find the Brothers of Gundar, and asks Bruno and Dian to accompany him on the road. He says that they may be gone for a day or so, and that there are always people in town in need of help.
He parts with a recommendation to visit Barthen, the trader when they get a chance.

#### At the Shrine of Neimín

Thom approaches the Shrine of Neimín and offers her help.
Sister Garaele, an aged Gnomish woman caring for the shrine, seeing and apparently quite young Dragonborn offfering a hand asks her to sweep up leaves.  Frustrated that she isnt being taken seriously for her apparent young age she boasts that she is quite capable of going on adventures.
Eventually Gruknar joins her at the shrine. 
Sister Garaele, invites Barbara, a tall woman sitting by the shrine to join her. She explains that she needs a group to accompany her Acolyte along the south road to  the abandoned town of Lios  Bearna and then make East for the forest and find the hut of the Banshee Agatha, there they must make an offering to Agatha of a Silver Comb and ask her for the Location of the Lost Manuscript of Neimín.
Gruknar offers a simple prayer at the shrine of Neimín before departing.

#### The Road South and Lios Bearna

Gruknar, Thom and Barb begin to head out of town but stop in on Barthan on their way. He provides them with some healing potions as additional payment for their help, and with a request that if they are heading south to Lios Bearna, some miners had reported seeing undead creatures near the ruins of the Old Owl Well.

The group head out along the south road and after some hours travel come to the abandoned town of Lios Bearna. On a hill to the West of the town the spot a trickle of smoke sprouting form the trees. This is in the direction that Barthan had indicated the Old Owl Well was.
The Party wondered why undead would need to light a fire.
Sticking to their primary mission, they turn East and head toward Coill Deargadh. Thom is aware she is perhaps less than a days journey from home, her village being just on the south border of the Forest.
They push on in to the forest. Thom is aware of stories of the Banshee, and knows that they are full of pride, but rarely dangerous unless insulted greatly.

#### Agatha's Hut
Arriving eventually deep in the forest they group find Agatha's hut. A simple door placed among a series of thick and intertwining roots.  They knock at the door multiple times and call out to Agatha with no response. Getting frustrated Gruknar considers blasting the door. Barb simply tries the door handle and the door opens with no resistance.
Inside is some dusty and battered furniture. Against the back wall is a vanity with a cracked mirror and an intricate silvered jewelry box.
They call out again to Agatha wishing to speak and get no response.
Barbara places the silver comb in the jewelry box when suddenly the room grows icy cold. A spectral form appears and greats Barbara kindly as a friend,
They ask Agatha about the Lost Manuscript and she responds that The lost manuscript is deep in the heart of Sleibh na nDeithe, the Mountains of the Gods. Where the source of the great river quenches the fires of Riarann. With that she departs.

#### The Old Owl Well
The party returns to Lios Bearna in the late afternoon. and Decide to  check out the reports of Undead at the Old Owl Well. They head into the woods and eventually come to a clearing. Up a hill by the ruins of a tower they spot a number of Zombies.

They begin moving up the hill attempting to get closer to the ruins and the zombies let out a cry and begin to move towards them. Sensing danger the party begins to attack the zombies.
Out of the ruins runs a man in red robes calling for the party to stop, he attempts to cast Charm Person on the Raging Barb. Gruknar recognises the symbol tattooed on the man as some necromantic symbol, while Thom, raised as a druid, knows that Zombies are raised corpses, an affront to the natural cycle of life and death.
Gruknar fires an Eldritch Blast at the Necromancer, while Thom spits acid at him. Finally Barb slays the necromancer with a killing blow.  The spell breaks with the Necromancers death and the zombies crumble and fall.

In the inner courtyard of the ruined tower the group find a tent belonging to the Necromancer, and a desk with some necromatic symbols, books and components.
They find a diary belonging to the Necromancer Hamun Kost. 

Hamun Kost’s tent contains a comfortable traveling suite, including a cot, a chair, a writing desk, supplies, and a chest of clothes. In the chest is a leather bag containing 35 sp, 20 ep, 20 gp, 5 pp, one pearl (100 gp), a _[potion of healing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/71-potion-of-healing)_, a [spell scroll](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5418-spell-scroll) of [darkness](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2058-darkness) in a bone tube, and a tiny jeweled box (25 gp) containing a [ring of protection](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4726-ring-of-protection) from ancient Netheril, the Red Wizard’s most interesting discovery so far. 10 doses of [[Arcane Condensate]]

Gruknar resists the urge to take some Condensate. The party decides to sleep in the relativee safety of the ruins before making their way back to Caer Ciaran the next day.

Gruknar dreams he is standing at the Shrine of Neimín where he offered a prayer that morning. The shrine begins to crumble and break, and he hears a voice. One that he first heard speaking to him through a hole in a wall in Bumford. It awoke him with a single word, rattling in his head, "Consume!"
